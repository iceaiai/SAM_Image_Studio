Calibration and statistics#

Confirm the physical scale#

Microscopy readers retain supported physical metadata. If it is missing, use Statistics → Edit image calibration… for X/Y sizes and a length unit. Do not interpret a pixel area as a physical area without a valid scale.

For batch lookup, choose Read pixel sizes from Excel…, select the worksheet and unit, and supply these header columns:

converted_image_name

converted_size

Sample_07.png

0.25

With unit nm, that row sets 0.25 nm/pixel in both directions for matching Sample_07.tiff, .dm3, or another extension. Matching removes directories and the last extension and ignores case, while retaining internal dots. Conflicting sizes for one normalized name are rejected. Unmatched images keep their existing scale.

The lookup is remembered for later opens, including after native restore. Forget automatic Excel lookup removes the association and retains scales already assigned. Changing calibration does not change masks; rotated images rebuild the physical coordinate basis from their saved transforms.

Explore object and group measurements#

Open Plot Object Statistics… and choose up to nine metrics for display. Adjust plot size, axes, labels, intervals, and colors. Export a plot or choose all/a subset of numerical metrics independently of the nine-metric display.

Metric family

Examples

Size and shape

Area, equivalent diameter, Feret diameter, perimeter, major/minor dimensions, aspect ratio, circularity, solidity

Position and orientation

Centroid, major-axis orientation, border status

Polygon morphology

Equal-area side estimate, corner quality, edge deviation

Core–shell / hollow

Explicitly linked core/cavity size, thickness, center displacement and uniformity

Identification and quality

Object ID, particle count, recorded model score

Summaries

Count, mean, median, standard deviation and distributions

Morphology / inner region… assigns morphology and links a valid fully enclosed core/cavity. A linked inner region is not counted as a second outer particle. A missing or invalid relationship produces notes/blank values, not invented thickness. Model confidence is not a calibrated probability; manual objects may have no score.

Shell/wall thickness is a 2-D boundary-distance estimate, not a 3-D measurement. The full manual defines each metric, formula, unit, and applicability rule.
